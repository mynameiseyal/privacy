Proteus — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Proteus is a protein & calorie tracker for Android. Your food logs stay on your device — we operate no server and have no accounts. Proteus does, however, show ads, and ads involve Google collecting some information about your device. This policy explains exactly what stays local, what leaves your device, and what choices you have.

Short version

  • No accounts. Proteus never asks who you are.
  • Your food data stays on your device. Entries, goals, saved default foods, and theme are stored in the app's private storage. We never receive them.
  • We run no server and no analytics of our own. There is nowhere for us to send your data to.
  • Proteus shows ads via Google AdMob. Google receives your device's advertising identifier and technical data in order to serve them. This is the one meaningful way data leaves your device without you typing anything.
  • Food searches you type are sent to nutrition services to look up values.
  • You can remove the ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

Data stored on your device

Your daily food entries, protein/calorie goal, saved default foods, and theme preference are stored locally in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device except through features you explicitly trigger:

  • Backup export/import — you can save this data to a file you choose, and restore it later. The file goes only where you send it.
  • Android system backup — if you have Android's backup enabled, the operating system may include this data in your device backup. That is handled by Android/Google under their terms, not by us.

To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Because we hold nothing, there is no server-side copy to request or erase.

Advertising

Proteus displays a banner ad and, at most once per day on launch, a full-screen app-open ad. These are served by Google AdMob.

To serve and measure ads, Google and its ad partners collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) — a resettable ID used to limit repeat ads and measure performance;
  • Device and technical data — device model, operating-system version, language/region, coarse location inferred from your IP address, and ad-interaction events such as impressions and clicks.

This information goes to Google, not to us. We see only aggregate, anonymous reporting (for example, "how many impressions yesterday") — never anything that identifies you or your device. Your food logs, goals, and presets are never shared with Google or any ad partner; the advertising SDK has no access to them.

For how Google handles this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices about ads

ChoiceWhat it does
Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) If you are in a region that requires it, Proteus shows a Google-provided consent form the first time you open the app, and ads are not loaded until you answer. You can choose non-personalized ads, which are based on context rather than a profile of your interests. You can change your answer at any time via Privacy options in the app's settings.
Reset or delete your advertising ID Android lets you reset the advertising ID, or delete it entirely so apps receive no ID at all: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device and Android version).
Remove ads A one-time in-app purchase permanently removes all ads from the app. If you buy it, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play — we never receive or store your name, card, or billing details. We only learn that this installation is entitled to an ad-free experience.

Network use and third parties

Apart from ads, Proteus contacts the network only to look up information you ask for. Most food lookups are answered offline from a public-domain USDA FoodData Central dataset bundled inside the app — no request leaves your device. When a food isn't in that dataset, or when you save a default food:

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
CalorieNinjas The food name you typed Fetch protein and calorie values
Open Food Facts The food name you typed Fallback lookup for foods and regional or branded terms CalorieNinjas lacks
Iconify The food name you typed Fetch a matching icon for a saved default food
Google Play Standard Play requests Check whether an app update is available, and process a "Remove ads" purchase if you make one

These requests are sent over HTTPS and contain only the search text plus what the network necessarily reveals (such as your IP address). They carry no account, name, or device identifier from us, and are not linked to your food history. We do not receive, log, or store them — each service handles the request under its own privacy policy.

Data we collect ourselves

None. We operate no server, use no analytics SDK, and use no crash-reporting SDK. The only data collection associated with Proteus is Google's, for advertising, as described above.

Children

Proteus is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them. The app is rated for a general audience and its ad settings are not configured to treat users as children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes affecting advertising or data sharing will also be reflected in the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration.
