Delete your Vacation Planner account

App: Vacation Planner (com.evworks.vacationplanner) · Publisher: EVWorks.Dev

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Vacation Planner and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — by email (no app needed)

Send an email to evworks.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Vacation Planner account", from the email address you signed up with (so we can confirm the account is yours). We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password hash)
  • All saved searches, including travellers' ages and budgets
  • Price history and records of alerts sent
  • Push-notification tokens for your devices

Deletion is permanent and complete — we keep no copy of this data. Routine hosting logs (such as request timestamps) expire on their own within a few days.

Delete some of your data (keep your account)

In the app: open Saved Searches and delete a search. The search — including its travellers' ages and budget — its price history and its alert records are deleted immediately and permanently.

By email: write to evworks.dev@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, saying which data you want deleted (for example: all saved searches, price history, or push tokens). We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm. Your account stays active.

See also the privacy policy.
