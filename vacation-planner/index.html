Vacation Planner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · App: com.evworks.vacationplanner · Publisher: EVWorks.Dev

Vacation Planner tracks flight prices for trips you save and notifies you when a fare drops under your budget. To do that it needs an account and has to store your saved searches on our server. The app is free and shows ads. This policy explains what we store, who else sees any of it, what the ad provider collects, and how to delete it.

Short version

  • An account is required — an email address and password.
  • We store your saved searches (routes, dates, travellers' ages, budget) so we can check prices for you twice a day.
  • Flight-price lookups go to Travelpayouts/Aviasales with the route, dates and currency only — never your email, name or account ID.
  • Push notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google).
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob. Google receives your device's advertising ID and technical data to serve them; your searches and account are never shared with Google or any ad partner.
  • No analytics of our own, and we never sell data.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time from the app, or from this website.

Where it is stored

Account and search data are stored with Supabase (our database and login provider) in the EU (Frankfurt). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access to each user's data is restricted to that user by database row-level security.

If you tap a flight to book it, the booking site opens in your browser and its own privacy policy applies. We are not involved in, and receive nothing from, that booking.

Advertising

Vacation Planner shows a banner ad on its main screens and, at most once per day when you return to the app, a full-screen ad. These are served by Google AdMob.

To serve and measure ads and to detect fraud, Google and its ad partners collect and process the following — this list follows Google's own disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK:

  • Identifiers — your device's Android advertising ID (resettable and deletable by you) and its app set ID;
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your device's general location;
  • Product interactions — app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic information — app and SDK performance such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.

Google states these are used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and that the data is encrypted in transit. This information goes to Google, not to us; we see only aggregate, anonymous reporting. Your account, saved searches, travellers' ages and price history are never shared with Google or any ad partner — the advertising SDK has no access to them.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the first time you open the app you see a Google-provided consent form, and no ad is loaded until you answer. You can choose non-personalised ads, based on context rather than a profile of your interests, and change your answer at any time via Privacy options in the app's Settings.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing (US states): if you live in a US state with a privacy law covering this, use the same Privacy options entry to tell us not to "sell" or "share" your information for targeted advertising. Ads keep showing; they stop being targeted using your information.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.
  • No location access, contacts, photos or other device data.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data. Apart from the advertising described above, we do not share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, saved searches, price history, alert records and push tokens.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on the account deletion page.
  • To delete a single search, delete it in the app's Saved Searches list.

Routine server logs kept by our hosting provider (for example, request timestamps) expire on their own within a few days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its processing. Most of this you can do directly in the app; for anything else, email us. We process your account and search data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), and advertising data on the basis of your consent where the law requires it, otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free app.

Children

Vacation Planner is not directed to children under 13, and accounts are for adults planning trips. A child's age may be entered as a traveller on an adult's search, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date, and material changes will be reflected in the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration.
